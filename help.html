Getting Started
5 ARTICLES
How do I get started with MarketDebriefs?
▾

Getting started takes under 60 seconds. Step 1 Go to marketdebriefs.com and click Launch App. Step 2 Create a free account using your email. Step 3 Type any instrument into the search bar — ES, Gold, EUR/USD, BTC, Oil — and hit BRIEF ME.

Your first brief will be ready in under 30 seconds. No credit card needed to start.

What exactly does MarketDebriefs do?
▾

MarketDebriefs is a macro intelligence tool for active traders. Before you enter a trade, you type in the instrument you're about to trade and receive an instant AI-powered briefing covering:

— The current central bank stance and what it means for your instrument
— Live geopolitical risks affecting the market
— Upcoming high-impact events (CPI, NFP, Fed decisions) and why they move price
— The broader macro context driving current price action

Think of it as having an institutional macro analyst on call — available in seconds, before every trade.

Which instruments and markets are supported?
▾

MarketDebriefs covers 25+ instruments across all major asset classes:

Equity Indices: ES S&P 500, NQ NASDAQ, RTY Russell 2000, YM Dow Jones, DAX 40, Nikkei 225, FTSE 100, CAC 40

Metals: Gold XAU/USD, Silver XAG/USD, Copper HG

Energy: WTI Crude Oil, Brent Crude, Natural Gas

FX: EUR/USD, GBP/USD, USD/JPY, AUD/USD, USD/CAD, USD/CHF, DXY

Crypto: Bitcoin, Ethereum

Rates: 10Y Treasury Note · Volatility: VIX

You can also type in any custom instrument or stock ticker and the AI will do its best to provide context.

How many free briefs do I get?
▾

On the FREE plan you get 3 Full Briefs per day. The limit resets every 24 hours.

If you need more briefs, or want access to Scalper Mode and Equity Debriefs, you can upgrade to PRO for €49/month with no daily limit.

Can I install MarketDebriefs on my phone?
▾

Yes — MarketDebriefs is a Progressive Web App (PWA), which means you can install it directly to your home screen without going through an app store.

iPhone (Safari): Tap the Share button → scroll down → tap Add to Home Screen.

Android (Chrome): Tap the three-dot menu → tap Add to Home Screen. Or tap the ⊕ GET APP button inside the app header.

Once installed it works like a native app — full screen, fast, and available from your home screen with one tap.

Features

What is Scalper Mode and how does it work?
▾

Scalper Mode is a PRO feature designed for short-term and intraday traders who need a fast macro risk check before entering a trade.

Instead of a full brief, you get a GREEN / YELLOW / RED risk signal in seconds:

🟢 GREEN — Macro conditions are clear. No imminent high-impact events.
🟡 YELLOW — Proceed with caution. Something is close on the calendar or in the news.
🔴 RED — Hold off. A major event or significant risk is imminent.

It also shows breaking news headlines and upcoming events due in the next hour or session. Think of it as a 10-second macro gut check before you pull the trigger.

How do Equity (Stocks) Debriefs work?
▾

Equity Debriefs are a PRO feature. Go to the Stocks tab, type any stock name or ticker (Apple, NVDA, TSLA, MSFT, etc.) and get an instant debrief covering:

— Earnings context — recent results and outlook
— Macro tailwinds — factors working in this stock's favour right now
— Macro headwinds — risks to watch
— Upcoming catalyst events — earnings dates, Fed decisions affecting the sector
— Sector rotation — is money flowing into or out of this sector?
— Institutional flow signals — what the smart money is doing

What is the Trade Journal / Reflection tab?
▾

The Reflection tab is a daily trading journal built into the app. At the end of each trading day, it presents you with 6 reflection prompts designed to help you build self-awareness around your trading decisions.

Questions like: "What did the market try to teach you today?" and "What emotion showed up most in your trading?"

Consistent journaling is one of the highest-leverage habits a trader can build. It's free for all users.

What is the "Learn to Fish" section?
▾

Learn to Fish is a free educational library of macro concepts that explain the forces behind market moves. Topics include:

— Why high-impact news moves markets
— The role of the US Dollar in everything
— Risk-on vs Risk-off flows
— How interest rates affect currencies
— Reading news like a trader
— Options flow and dealer gamma

The goal is not just to give you briefings — but to help you understand why markets move the way they do.

How current and accurate is the data in briefs?
▾

Each brief is generated fresh on demand using an AI model with broad macro and market knowledge. The briefings focus on current macro themes, central bank stances, and upcoming scheduled events — not historical recaps or stale data.

For best results, run a fresh brief before each trading session rather than relying on a brief from hours ago. Markets move fast.

Important: MarketDebriefs is an intelligence and research tool. It does not constitute financial advice and should be used as part of your broader trading process, not as a standalone signal.

Your Briefs
3 ARTICLES
My brief failed or returned an error — what should I do?
▾

Brief errors are usually temporary. Try these steps in order:

1 Wait 10–15 seconds and try again — occasional timeouts resolve themselves.
2 Check your internet connection.
3 Try a different instrument to rule out a specific query issue.
4 Refresh the page and try again.

If the issue persists, contact us at support@marketdebriefs.com and include the instrument you searched and the error message shown.

My brief count seems wrong — it shows I've used more than I have.
▾

The brief counter increments when a brief is successfully returned, not when you press the button. If a brief returned an error, it should not count against your daily limit.

If you believe your count is incorrect, contact support with your account email and we'll look into it and restore any lost credits.

Can I save or review past briefs?
▾

Brief history is not currently stored between sessions — each brief is generated fresh on demand. This keeps the intelligence current rather than showing you stale data from hours ago.

If you want to refer back to a brief during a session, simply keep the tab open. Brief saving and history is a feature we're considering for a future update.

Which browsers and devices does MarketDebriefs support?
▾

MarketDebriefs works on all modern browsers — Chrome, Safari, Firefox, and Edge — on both desktop and mobile.

For the best mobile experience, install it as a PWA (see "Can I install MarketDebriefs on my phone?" above). The PWA experience is optimised for iOS Safari and Android Chrome.

Internet Explorer is not supported.

Does MarketDebriefs work offline?
▾

The app shell loads offline, but generating briefs requires an internet connection since they are generated live by the AI on each request.

The Trade Journal and Learn to Fish sections work fully offline once the app has been loaded at least once.

The app feels slow — how can I improve performance?
▾

Brief generation typically takes 5–15 seconds depending on query complexity. If things feel slower than usual:

1 Check your internet connection speed.
2 Try installing the app as a PWA — the native-like experience is noticeably faster than a browser tab.
3 Clear your browser cache and reload.

If you're consistently experiencing slowness, let us know at support@marketdebriefs.com with your device and browser details.
